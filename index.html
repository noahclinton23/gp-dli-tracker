Only players who are on the floor for a defensive possession will appear in the post game report. If you try to refresh/close with stats entered, you’ll get an “Are you sure?” warning.

1) Check the “On Floor” boxes for your current 5.
2) Press “Mark Defensive Possession” each time your team plays a defensive possession.
3) Only those players will show in the report.
